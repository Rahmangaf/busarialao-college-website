# Boarding and student life

Source: PDF Chapter 3 (pp.46 to 62). All items were checked against the body text of the pages cited.

## Boarding (PDF p.61)

- Boarding builds discipline and independence: students learn to wake early, attend prep, do lab work and follow daily routines.
- **2010:** the hostel was established at **Osere**, supervised by Mr Shedrac Agweda and Mrs Abdullateef Aminat with four other staff.
- **2013:** relocated to **Yidi Road**, accommodating almost **80 students**.
- **Today:** ten hostel staff handle welfare, safety, discipline and guidance, including academic and moral support.
- The hostel is described as homely, with good catering, healthcare and social provision, and **24-hour electricity through solar power**.
- Show both dates (2010 at Osere; 2013 at Yidi Road).

### Sample details used in the design **[SAMPLE]**

- Daily routine: 5:30 wake-up and morning devotion; 6:30 breakfast and prep; 7:45 classes; 4:00 clubs, sport and rest; 7:00 supervised study; 9:30 lights out.

## School cafeteria (PDF p.62)

Open to students and staff, serving healthy, affordable meals with variety, snacks and drinks; described as clean and well managed. Table-manners messages are displayed.

## Clubs and societies (PDF pp.53 to 54)

Seven clubs: Health Science, Life Science, Model Youth, Physical Science, Readers, Press, and Drama. Each has a teacher coordinator and student executives. The design lists different clubs (Mathematics, Debate, ICT, Science) **[CONFIRM; use the seven]**.

## Sports and fitness (PDF pp.58 to 59)

Every student takes part in at least one physical activity weekly. Football is the most popular sport (inter-class, friendly and annual inter-house matches). Also athletics and track events, indoor games (chess, scrabble, darts, table tennis) and PHE sessions. Facilities: football pitch, volleyball, basketball and badminton courts, table tennis and fitness equipment. Weekly structure: staff exercise Wednesday 7:00 to 7:35; learners' sport Wednesday 2:00 to 4:00 pm; hostelites' aerobics Saturday and Sunday 6:30 to 7:30 am with a trained fitness expert. Teams compete at zonal, local government and state level and have won medals in football, athletics and indoor games.

## Character and leadership (PDF pp.45 to 52)

- **Guidance and counselling (p.45):** orientation for new students, academic and career counselling, personal and social support, exam preparation, moral re-orientation, and work with parents. Led by the school counsellor.
- **Moral and character education (pp.46 to 47):** discipline, integrity and honesty, respect and tolerance, responsibility, hard work. Delivered through the code of conduct, assemblies, counselling, staff role-modelling and clubs.
- **Leadership and mentorship (pp.47 to 48):** student council (Head Boy and Head Girl, Sports Captain, Club Heads); science clubs; two-week leadership training and certification at **Sea School, Apapa, Lagos**; teacher, alumni and industry mentors. Sea School is an excursion for **SS2** students (school confirmed).
- **Field trips (pp.48 to 52):** Old Oyo National Park (May 2007, the first field trip); Republic of Benin (first in August 2010, now for SS3: Porto-Novo, Cotonou, Ouidah); Sea School Lagos; Erin-Ijesa Waterfall; International Aviation College, Ilorin; Kainji Lake National Park (yearly, JSS3 and SS1); International Tobacco Company; and others including Esie museum, Arinta Falls, Ikogosi Warm Spring, Idanre Hill, University of Ilorin and local industries. **[CONFIRM before listing the tobacco company on a school site]**
- **Staff welfare (p.60):** an internal topic (allowances, recognition awards, pension scheme, training). Not for the public site.
