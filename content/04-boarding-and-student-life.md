## Boarding

- Boarding helps students manage time between academics, practical work and personal activities, building discipline and independence.
- The hostel opened in **2010 at Osere**, supervised by a small team of staff.
- In **2013** it moved to **Yidi Road** with almost **80 students**.
- Today the hostel has **10 staff** responsible for welfare, safety, discipline and guidance, including academic and moral support.
- Show both dates (2010 at Osere, 2013 at Yidi Road) wherever the hostel history appears.
- Weekend routine in the magazine: hostelites do aerobic exercise on Saturday and Sunday, 6:30 to 7:30 a.m., under a trained fitness instructor.

## Sports and fitness

- **Facilities:** a football pitch; volleyball, basketball and badminton courts; table tennis equipment; fitness and aerobic materials.
- **Activities:** football (inter-class, friendlies, annual inter-house), athletics (sprints, long-distance, javelin, high jump, relays), indoor games (chess, scrabble, darts, table tennis), and PE health sessions.
- **Competitions:** zonal, local-government and state level.
- **Weekly structure:** learners' sporting activities Wednesdays 2:00 to 4:00 p.m.; staff morning exercise Wednesdays 7:00 to 7:35 a.m.; staff volleyball Fridays 4:00 to 6:00 p.m.

## Character and leadership

- Core moral values promoted: discipline and self-control, integrity and honesty, respect and tolerance, responsibility and accountability, hard work and perseverance.
- Methods: school rules and code of conduct, morning assemblies and talks, guidance and counselling, staff role modelling, co-curricular activities.
- Leadership: student council (Head Boy/Girl, Sports Captain, Club Heads), science clubs, student-led projects, and a two-week certified leadership training at Sea School, Apapa, Lagos.
- Mentorship: teacher mentors, alumni mentorship, industry visits.
