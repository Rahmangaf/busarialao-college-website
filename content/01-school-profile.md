- **Tagline (logo):** "…educating to inspire"
- **Anniversary theme:** "Nurturing Scientific Minds, Inspiring the Future"
- **Founded:** the college opened in **2006**. It grew out of **Top Tutors**, a tutorial centre begun in **2003**.
- **Type:** science-focused secondary school, JSS1 to SS3, day and boarding **[CONFIRM levels and co-ed status]**

## Our story (PDF pp.20 to 24)

- **2003:** Top Tutors began in a modest two-room apartment at Adewole Estate, Ilorin. It prepared students for external exams with specialist science teachers.
- **The name:** the founder chose his father's name, Alhaji Busari Alao Gafar Adua, who died in 1992, fourteen years before the first classroom was built.
- **2006:** Alhaji Yunus Ajibola Gafar founded Busarialao College at age 43, after twenty years in the civil service and with a professional accounting qualification. It opened with one block of classrooms, an administrative building, and laboratories for Chemistry, Physics, Biology and Agricultural Science.
- **First intake:** nine learners and nine staff (the pioneers).
- **Top Tutors** remained the academic arm: it sets exam questions (Saturday tests, certification exams, preparatory exams), trains teachers, and runs staff seminars.
- **The founder's vision:** "A school where science comes alive": practical, well-equipped, with dedicated teachers. His accounting background shaped the school's discipline, planning and financial management (p.23).
- **Early challenges:** the founder took loans to keep the school running, cut his own spending, and had difficulty finding qualified science tutors and persuading parents to trust a new school (p.24). The brochure says he was never motivated by profit.
- **Directors:** the late Alhaji Yunus Gafar, the late Alhaji Busari Alao, and Mallam Yinusa Tajudeen (p.21) Not needed on the public site: omit the directors list.

## Vision, mission and values (PDF p.22)

**Vision:** To be a leading Centre of academic excellence where young minds are inspired, empowered, and equipped with the knowledge, skills, and character needed to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

**Mission:**
- To provide a conducive learning environment that promotes quality education and intellectual curiosity.
- To nurture students through a balanced blend of strong scientific education, moral guidance, and life-building skills.
- To develop critical thinkers, problem-solvers, and innovators who can compete globally and contribute meaningfully to national development.
- To partner with parents, educators, and the community to shape responsible, confident, and well-rounded individuals.

**Core values:** Excellence, Discipline, Integrity, Hard Work, Innovation & Scientific Inquiry, Teamwork & Community, Leadership & Responsibility. The printed list (p.22) numbers them 1 to 5, 7 and 8: there is no value 6, so show these seven. Do not add Godliness & Moral Uprightness, which came from the earlier draft.

## Vision 2035 (PDF pp.98 to 100)

- **Vision:** to be a leading science institution known for academic excellence, strong discipline, innovation, and a sustained zero-tolerance culture towards examination malpractice, as upheld since its inception.
- **Mission:** to provide quality science education through dedicated teaching, modern facilities and high ethical standards, producing students who excel academically and uphold honesty, responsibility and service.
- Priorities: sustain academic and moral excellence; practical learning and critical thinking; moral and ethical values; strong leadership and staff commitment; student character development.
