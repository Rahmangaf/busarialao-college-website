## Our story

- **2003:** Began as **Top Tutors**, a tutorial centre in a two-room apartment at Adewole Estate, Ilorin. Students were prepared for external exams by specialist science teachers.
- **2005:** Top Tutors became Busarialao College, named in honour of the founder's father, Late Alhaji Busarialao, and moved to Irewolede, Ilorin. It opened with **12 pioneer students and 12 staff**.
- Top Tutors was kept as the college's academic arm. It sets exam questions (Saturday Tests, Certification Exams, Preparatory Exams), runs teacher training, and organises seminars for Local Government and State Ministry staff.
- **The founder's vision:** Alhaji Yunus Gafar, an accountant by profession, saw bright students losing interest in science because schools lacked laboratories, practical exposure and motivated teachers. He set out to build a school "where science comes alive". His accounting background shaped the school's discipline, planning and financial management. He was not motivated by profit. In the early years the school faced limited funds, low enrolment and difficulty finding qualified science tutors, and the founder borrowed to keep it running.
- **Directors:** Late Alhaji Yunus Gafar, Late Alhaji Busarialao, and Alhaji Yinusa Tajudeen.

## Vision, mission and values (magazine section 1.6)

**Vision:** To be a leading centre of academic excellence where young minds are inspired, empowered, and equipped with the knowledge, skills, and character needed to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

**Mission:**
- To provide a conducive learning environment that promotes quality education and intellectual curiosity.
- To nurture students through a balanced blend of strong scientific education, moral guidance, and life-building skills.
- To develop critical thinkers, problem-solvers, and innovators who can compete globally and contribute meaningfully to national development.
- To partner with parents, educators, and the community to shape responsible, confident, and well-rounded individuals.

**Core values:**
1. **Excellence:** the highest standards in academics, behaviour and personal growth.
2. **Discipline:** focused, respectful, punctual and responsible students.
3. **Integrity:** honesty and transparency in everything the school does.
4. **Hard Work:** success through dedication and diligence.
5. **Innovation & Scientific Inquiry:** creativity, experimentation and solving real-world problems.
6. **Godliness & Moral Uprightness:** strong moral values and God-consciousness.
7. **Teamwork & Community:** collaboration and mutual support.
8. **Leadership & Responsibility:** confident students ready to take responsibility.
